## Release blockers and honest limits

- The downloaded installer test ran on a real Windows host but not a clean Windows account or VM.
- A normal visible launch, installed-provider discovery, course creation, update exercise, and
  learner-data preservation check were not performed against these exact draft bytes. They remain
  the final learner-path smoke check rather than being inferred from source tests or verification
  mode.
- The release-signing environment contains the matching private key, but an encrypted offline
  recovery copy has not been confirmed.
- The Windows packages intentionally have no commercial operating-system code-signing certificate.
  Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning; the Ed25519 signature protects the Lerience update
  manifest and does not remove that warning.
- The draft remains unpublished.
