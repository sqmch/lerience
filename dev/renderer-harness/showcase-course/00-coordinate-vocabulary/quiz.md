# Recall — coordinates and displacement

1. `[3, 4]` could be a position or a displacement. What question does each one answer?
2. Which of these is meaningless, and why: position + displacement, position − position,
   position + position, displacement + displacement?
3. You want the walk from A to B. Is it `A − B` or `B − A`?
4. What does `[0, 0]` mean as a position? What does it mean as a displacement?
5. Why does this course use `[x, y]` arrays rather than `{ x, y }` objects?
6. A route is given as a list of legs. In one sentence, how do you find where it ends?
