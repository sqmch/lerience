## S16

A bug report:
A bug related to some node_modules deletion or something that occurred a couple of times in one session, not sure how widespread it is or how tied to the actual course it happened in, but worth a look. What follows is the self diagnosis response of the tutor itself:
"Found the mechanism. Before I report it, let me confirm it rather than assert it — throwaway dirs, nothing real at risk.

Confirmed, and it's worse than a race — it's deterministic.

Diagnosed, and it's an engine bug, not your machine.

scripts/qa-module.mjs copies your scaffold to a temp dir and points a Windows junction at your real node_modules so QA doesn't have to reinstall. Cleanup is supposed to remove the junction before deleting the temp tree — the comment at line 653 even says "so a recursive delete can never reach the real install it points at." But the removal it uses is itself fs.rmSync(link, { recursive: true, force: true }), and on Node 24 that follows the junction and empties the target:

isSymbolicLink: true | isDirectory: false
real dir exists after rmSync: true
real dir contents: []          ← payload file destroyed through the link
fs.unlinkSync(link) and fs.rmdirSync(link) both remove the junction and leave the target untouched. It's recursive: true that does the damage — the guard written to protect the install is the thing deleting it.

So every npm run qa on this module empties your scaffold's dependencies. That's all three occurrences, including the two the journal wrote off as "verified intact." Not flaky, not Windows being Windows — deterministic.

I can't patch it: scripts/ is engine, and the course rules bar me from editing engine files so Lerience can verify its own updates. It's a one-word fix at line 660 and worth reporting upstream. My side: I re-run npm ci and actually execute the checks after any QA run from now on, rather than writing "intact" in a note."
